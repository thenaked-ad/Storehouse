# Editing the Storehouse website

A short guide to changing the words and photographs on storehousefinearts.com yourself.

## Signing in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and choose **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Sign in as **storehousefinearts**. The first time, GitHub asks you to approve Pages CMS — click to allow it.
3. Choose the **Storehouse** project.

Down the left you'll see the pages: Home, Storage, Transport & Handling, Additional Services, About, Contact — and **Email & Instagram**. Click one to open it as a form laid out like the page.

## Changing words

Click into any box and type. A few things happen on their own:

- **Paragraphs** — leave a blank line between them.
- **Quotes and apostrophes** are turned into proper typographic ones when the site is published, so just type normally.
- **Formatting** — there isn't any to worry about. The site's own type and spacing are applied automatically, so everything matches.

## Photographs

Each section on Storage, Transport & Handling and Additional Services has its own set of **Photographs**, shown beside the text in the order listed.

- **Add one:** click **Add** under Photographs, then **Upload** (or pick one you've uploaded before).
- **Reorder:** drag a photograph up or down the list.
- **Remove:** use the bin icon on that photograph.
- **Description:** a short line saying what the photograph shows. It's read aloud to people who can't see it, and search engines use it.
- **Caption (optional):** shown in small type under the photograph. Leave it empty for none.

Any size of photograph is fine — the website makes a properly sized copy automatically. Use **JPEG**, **PNG** or **WebP**. iPhone photos sometimes save as HEIC: export those as JPEG first (in Photos, File → Export).

Photographs of artworks are always shown whole — never cropped.

## Sections

On Storage, Transport & Handling and Additional Services you can add a section, remove one, or drag them into a different order. The numbering (01.1, 01.2…) updates itself.

A section can have a **button** that opens an email to you with a subject already filled in. Fill in **Button** and **Button's email subject** to have one; leave Button empty for none.

## Saving

Click **Save**. The live site updates about a minute later — refresh the page to see it.

There is no separate draft step, so it's worth a quick look at the page after saving. Nothing is ever lost: every version is kept, so if something goes wrong, tell me and I can put it back in seconds.

## What stays the same

The layout, colours, fonts, menu and page titles aren't in the editor, so nothing you do there can affect the design. If you'd like any of those changed, just let me know.

The **Instagram** photographs on the home page update on their own from your account — there's nothing to do there.
