# Editing the Storehouse website

A short guide to changing the words and photographs on storehousefinearts.com yourself.

## Signing in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and choose **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Sign in as **storehousefinearts**. The first time, GitHub asks you to approve Pages CMS — click to allow it.
3. Choose the **Storehouse** project.

Down the left you'll see the pages: Home, Storage, Transport & Handling, Additional Services, About, Contact — and **Email & Instagram**. Click one to open it as a form laid out like the page.

## Changing words

Click into any box and type. Quotes and apostrophes are turned into proper typographic ones when the site is published, so just type normally.

In a section's **Text**, a few simple markers lay the text out for you:

| Type this | And you get |
|---|---|
| A blank line between paragraphs | Separate paragraphs |
| `- ` at the start of a line | A bullet point. Lines next to each other become one list. |
| `1. ` (any number) at the start of a line | A numbered step |
| A short line on its own, directly above its paragraph (no blank line between) | A small subheading |

In a bullet or step that opens with a few words and a full stop — *Arrival. Works are checked in…* — those opening words are picked out, so a list reads clearly at a glance.

For example:

```
Charged separately, only when needed and agreed in advance:

- Condition reports
- Photography

Perimeter and reception.
The site is enclosed by palisade fencing…
```

Everything else — fonts, sizes, spacing — is applied automatically, so it always matches the rest of the site.

## Photographs

Storage, Transport & Handling and Additional Services each have **Photographs**: the strip of photographs below the sections, which visitors drag (or swipe) sideways. They run left to right in the order listed. The About portrait and the Contact photograph are set on those pages.

- **Add one:** click **Add** under Photographs, then **Upload** (or pick one you've uploaded before).
- **Reorder:** drag a photograph up or down the list.
- **Remove:** use the bin icon on that photograph.
- **Description:** a short line saying what the photograph shows. It's read aloud to people who can't see it, and search engines use it.
- **Caption (optional):** shown in small type under the photograph — in the strips, and under the About portrait and the Contact photograph. Leave it empty for none.

Any size and shape of photograph is fine — the website makes properly sized copies automatically, and every photograph is shown whole, never cropped. Use **JPEG**, **PNG** or **WebP**. iPhone photos sometimes save as HEIC: export those as JPEG first (in Photos, File → Export). Larger originals look sharper: aim for at least 1600 pixels on the long side.

## Sections

On Storage, Transport & Handling and Additional Services you can add a section, remove one, or drag them into a different order. The numbering (01.1, 01.2…) updates itself.

A section can have a **button** that opens an email to you with a subject already filled in. Fill in **Button** and **Button's email subject** to have one; leave Button empty for none.

## Saving

Click **Save**. The live site updates about a minute later — refresh the page to see it.

There is no separate draft step, so it's worth a quick look at the page after saving. Nothing is ever lost: every version is kept, so if something goes wrong, tell me and I can put it back in seconds.

## What stays the same

The layout, colours, fonts, menu and page titles aren't in the editor, so nothing you do there can affect the design. If you'd like any of those changed, just let me know.

The **Instagram** photographs on the home page update on their own from your account — there's nothing to do there.
