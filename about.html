About

Our experienced team comes from the art world, and we handle every work the way a gallery would.

Storehouse is a single, joined-up service. The same people who condition-check a work pack it, move it, photograph it and hang it — which is why nothing gets lost between one stage and the next. We work from a secure, climate-controlled facility in Acton, north-west London.
