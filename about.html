About

Our experienced team comes from the art world, and we handle every work the way a gallery would.

The team

Storehouse is a single, joined-up service. The same people who condition-check a work pack it, move it, photograph it and hang it — which is why nothing gets lost between one stage and the next.

How we work

We work from a secure, climate-controlled facility in Park Royal, north-west London, providing artists, galleries, collectors and estates with the infrastructure behind their exhibitions, fairs and collections.

We keep it competitive, and we keep it close.
