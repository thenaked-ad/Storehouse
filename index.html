Overview

Storehouse is a single, joined-up service covering fine art storage, transport, exhibition and collection management. We work from a secure, climate-controlled facility in north-west London, providing artists, galleries, collectors and estates with the infrastructure behind their exhibitions, fairs and collections.

Our experienced team comes from the art world, and we handle every work the way a gallery would.

We keep it competitive, and we keep it close.
